Features

Everything you need to
stay organized

BoxVise combines smart inventory management with powerful tools to help you track, find, and manage everything you own.

QR Code System

Generate unique QR codes for every box. Scan to instantly view contents, add items, or share with others.

Smart Search

Find any item across all your boxes instantly. Search by name, tags, description, or even location.

Secure & Private

All your data stays on your device. No cloud accounts needed. Your inventory, your control.

Export & Print

Export your inventory as PDF or CSV. Print QR label sheets for all your boxes in one click.

Tags & Categories

Organize items with custom tags. Smart suggestions auto-categorize based on what you add.

Reminders & Expiry

Set reminders for items and track expiry dates. Never forget what's in your boxes again.
